### Why it is flagged

The CNRS press page makes this image available for press/editorial use, but does not
grant an explicit free-use or Creative Commons licence for third-party portfolio websites.
The current caption notes the copyright holder; the image is reproduced for commentary
purposes pending formal confirmation.

### Action required

Contact the CNRS press office or Jean-Pierre Luminet directly to confirm reproduction
rights for a personal/commercial portfolio website:

- **CNRS press**: presse@cnrs.fr
- **Luminet's institutional page**: https://www.luth.obspm.fr/luth2/spip.php?article47
- **Alternative**: confirm whether the image has been released under a CC licence and
  cite the licence identifier (e.g. CC BY 4.0) in the caption.

If rights cannot be confirmed, replace with the 2019 EHT photograph of M87*
(CC BY 4.0, Event Horizon Telescope Collaboration) or another freely-licensed
black-hole image.
